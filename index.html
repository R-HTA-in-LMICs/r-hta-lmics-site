R for HTA in LMICs

A global initiative supporting health economists and decision modellers in low- and middle-income countries through open-source R tools.

We help HTA analysts in LMICs build cost-effectiveness models, improve reproducibility, and develop transferable analytical skills — all using freely available R packages.

Who We Are

R-HTA in LMICs is the regional chapter of the global R-HTA consortium. We promote the use of R for health technology assessment in LMICs through open-access tutorials, workshops, and curated resources.
